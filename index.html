Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Audit

    I review your store, theme, and apps to find what is slowing pages down or costing you sales.

  2. 02

    Build

    I build on a duplicate of your theme, so your live store keeps selling untouched while the work happens.

  3. 03

    Review

    You get a preview link to test on your own devices, and I revise until it’s right.

  4. 04

    Launch

    The approved work goes live, I check it on the real store, and I stay available for follow-up fixes.
